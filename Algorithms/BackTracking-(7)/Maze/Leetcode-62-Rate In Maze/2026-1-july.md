Think of `grid[i][j]` not as "how we got here," but as **"how many ways are there to reach the destination *from* this cell."**

```text
🟥 START [0,0] ───► 🟡 CELL [1,1] ───► 🟩 DESTINATION [m-1,n-1]
                      │ (Calculated ONCE: 
                      ▼  "There are 5 ways to get from here to End")

```

Once the first path calculates that there are exactly 5 ways to reach the destination from that specific yellow cell, any future path reaching that cell can instantly read `5` and turn around immediately, saving you from recalculating the exact same routes down and right!
