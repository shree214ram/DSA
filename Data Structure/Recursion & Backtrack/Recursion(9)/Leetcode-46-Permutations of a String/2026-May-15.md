It’s a great instinct to connect **Permutations (L46)** to **Next Permutation (L31)**, but here is the big "Aha!" moment for your FAANG prep:

**Recursion is actually the wrong tool for Next Permutation.**

While L46 asks for *all* possibilities (which requires the Backtracking/Recursion you just mastered), L31 asks for the very next lexicographical order. If you use recursion to find all permutations and then sort them, your time complexity will be $O(N!)$, which will **fail** a FAANG interview.

### **The Hint for Next Permutation (The "Peak & Valley" Logic)**

To solve this in $O(N)$ time, think of the numbers as a mountain range.

1. **Find the "Pivot":** Scan from **right to left** to find the first number that is smaller than the one to its right. (This is the first place where the "increasing" order breaks). Let’s call this index `i`.
2. **Find the "Successor":** Scan from **right to left** again to find the first number larger than `nums[i]`. Let’s call this index `j`.
3. **Swap:** Swap `nums[i]` and `nums[j]`.
4. **Reverse:** Everything to the right of index `i` is currently in descending order. To get the *next* smallest permutation, you must **reverse** that entire right section to make it ascending.
